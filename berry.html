Inspiration

For the millions of women who track their cycle, the experience is often negative, clinical, and tedious. A study showed that apps were often designed to help users keep track of their current health rather than improve it (National Library of Medicine, 2023). On top of that, we learned that up to 90% of women experience mood-related PMS symptoms, creating a clear need for better mental wellness tools.

A pivotal Oxford University study showed that simple, engaging games can be beneficial for well-being. This was our lightbulb moment: we could combine the utility of a tracker with the gentle, rewarding nature of an idle game to create an experience that was genuinely positive and affirming.

What It Does

Berry is an AI-powered digital garden that reframes your menstrual cycle as a journey of self-care. Nurture a personal plant that grows and rests in sync with your body's natural rhythm.

How It Works

  • Log Your Feelings: Each day, you can log your mood and symptoms with a simple interface.
  • Nurture Your Plant: Your daily check-in acts as a way to "water" or tend to your plant, creating a positive feedback loop. The plant's visual stage—from a new sprout to a flowering or fruit-bearing plant—reflects your current cycle phase.
  • Receive AI-Powered Empathy: Using the Google Gemini API, Berry generates personalized, empathetic affirmations and wellness tips based on your cycle phase and logged feelings. It also features a reliable AI chatbot for any questions, providing supportive and gentle guidance.

We turned a data-heavy interface into a soothing, gamified ritual designed to empower women and de-stigmatize the menstrual cycle.

How We Built It

We built Berry in under 48 hours thanks to the help of vibe coding (<3 Cursor and Co-pilot).

  • Frontend: HTML and CSS
  • Backend: JavaScript paired with the Gemini API

Challenges & Accomplishments

Our chatbot feature using the Gemini API would not work at first. It turned out we were hitting the rate limit, so we implemented backup code to work around this issue.
