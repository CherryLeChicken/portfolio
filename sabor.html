Sabor Cafe

​Sabor is a social roleplay experience on Roblox. At Sabor Cafe, players can step into the roles of barista or customer, interact with others, and create their favorite café treats, including bubble tea, coffee, and a range of desserts.

Contributions

  • Collaborated closely with modelers, builders, and scripters to bring the concept to life

Sabor Cafe Over The Years

2019 2021 Today

V1: Where it all started, everything was built from scratch. I took the initative to learn how to script, build, and design UI in Roblox Studio to make our first cafe game.

V2: Also known as the cardboard box. For this version, I experimented a lot with the UI and scalability on different devices. In this era, we also began hosting community events on Roblox and Discord to grow our group.

Today: With the success of V2, we had more resources to invest in elevating our experience. We collaborated with a builder to bring our vision of a dark, industrial café to life, and worked with a scripter to create a more interactive, engaging kitchen environment.

Run from Kpop memes at 2am

Conclusion
