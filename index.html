About Me

My journey into design and development began with one goal: to build my own Roblox café. With no budget to hire a team, I taught myself UI design, coding, and game development through countless YouTube tutorials and Google searches. After months of trial and error, I co-founded my own Roblox group, Sabor, and built a fully functioning café game that players could explore, interact, and enjoy.

Through this process, I gained hands-on experience in game design, user experience design, and collaboration. I learned how to create intuitive interfaces, write efficient scripts, and bring ideas to life in a digital space. More importantly, this experience opened my eyes to the combination of design, technology, and business—leading me to pursue Global Business and Digital Arts at the University of Waterloo. Now, I apply that same resourcefulness to creating engaging digital products, always ready to tackle new challenges and turn ideas into reality!
