BeGym

January 2025 - April 2025

BeGym is a beginner-friendly fitness app designed to encourage beginners to build healthy habits and stay motivated. It was designed during my BET 350 Customer Experience Design class in a group of 6 during Winter 2025.

Course
BET 350
Team Size

Project Overview

Starting a fitness journey can feel overwhelming. BeGym was created to support beginners by removing barriers, like fear of judgment and lack of guidance, and turning fitness into a fun, social, and rewarding experience. We combined AI-powered coaching, habit-building gamification, and community support to help users take the first step and keep going.

User Research

We surveyed 10 university students to uncover key pain points and needs. Some of the key findings came from the following survey questions:

  • Most students aimed for muscle gain, endurance, and general health.
  • Flexibility and weight loss were also common secondary goals.
  • Health concerns, stress relief, and body image were top motivators.
  • Some were driven by productivity and forming healthy habits.
  • Lack of time and motivation due to school/work schedules.
  • Fear of judgment and not knowing how to use gym equipment properly.
  • Limited access to transportation or affordable gyms.
  • Form correction and real-time feedback ranked highest.
  • Personalized workout plans and motivational reminders were also popular.
  • Some users requested diet tips and gym crowd level predictions.
  • Most students preferred video instructions from YouTube or TikTok.
  • Others liked one-on-one guidance or graphic workout sheets.
  • Written workout plans were helpful for goal tracking.

Customer Journey Map

We mapped the full user experience in a Customer Journey app from app discovery to routine building.

Then we ideated features and prioritized features that would reduce intimidation, boost motivation, and build consistency.

Key Features

  • AI Form Checker: Easily get real-time feedback on your exercise form using your device's camera
  • AI Chat Gym Coach: Personalized guidance and motivation through an AI-powered chat coach
  • Community Tab & Leaderboards: Connect with others, share progress, and compete on leaderboards
  • Widgets & Notifications: Stay on track with workout reminders and progress widgets
  • Fit Flora Plant: Grow your own virtual plant as you complete workouts and build healthy habits

Contributions

I played a key role in both the research and design for Begym. I led the user research phase by creating and distributing a survey to university students in our BET 350 class to uncover their fitness goals, habits, and challenges. After collecting responses, I organized the data into key themes that helped shape our solution. I contributed to the customer journey map to visualize the user experience and identify opportunities for Begym. Inspired by Duolingo’s motivational tactics, I proposed the idea for Fit Flora, a plant that grows as users complete workouts, to gamify progress and encourage consistency. I also suggested adding a community feature to support social motivation. On the design side, I prototyped the Widgets & Notifications page and the AI Chat page.

Reflection

Working on BeGym taught me the importance of empathy in design, especially for beginners who may feel overwhelmed by fitness apps. Our group's focus on encouragement, community, and positive reinforcement helped us create an app that makes fitness approachable and rewarding for everyone.
