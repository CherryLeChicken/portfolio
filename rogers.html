Beyond the Seat Brand Package

Developed a Rogers "Beyond the Seat" Brand Package at the uXperience Design Jam at the Stratford School of Design and Interaction.

View Presentation

Design Challenge

The design challenge for the Rogers UXperience Design Jam centered around enhancing visibility and awareness of Rogers Beyond the Seat, a perks program that offers exclusive sports and entertainment experiences to Rogers customers. The prompt emphasized two key goals:

  1. Increasing visibility of the program so more customers are aware of and engage with the perks.
  2. Strengthening the connection between the program and the Rogers brand, making it clear that these exclusive experiences are a unique benefit of being a Rogers customer.

Our Solution

In a team of four, we designed a solution that not only showcases the value of the program but also aligned with Rogers' identity, reinforcing customer loyalty through memorable, branded experiences. The goal is to create positive, memorable interactions that leave people feeling excited and entertained. In return, they will associate those good feelings with Rogers. By tying the perks program to real-life experiences, we help build stronger brand presence and emotional connection.

Key Features

  • Partner with lifestyle influencers to increase the program's visibility.
  • Dedicate a page on the Rogers website to improve accessibility to the program's information.
  • Provide real-time notifications for contests, prizes, and events while offering exclusive in-app live streams with a trial of Rogers' 5G data.
  • Set up interactive booths in high-traffic locations like universities and transit stations, featuring games, exclusive prizes, VR experiences, and 5G demos.
