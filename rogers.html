Design Challenge

Rogers Beyond the Seat is a customer rewards program offering exclusive access to premier sports and entertainment experiences. Rogers customers can enter to win VIP experiences such as tickets to major concerts and sporting events, behind-the-scenes access like Blue Jays batting practice or NHL open skates, and meet-and-greets with celebrities and athletes. These unique opportunities are designed to enhance customer engagement and foster a deeper connection with the Rogers brand.

Despite its value proposition, the program suffered from low awareness. Only 2% of Rogers customers were aware of the program. Therefore the design prompt emphasized two key goals:

  1. Increasing visibility of the program so more customers are aware of and engage with the perks.
  2. Strengthening the connection between the program and the Rogers brand, making it clear that these exclusive experiences are a unique benefit of being a Rogers customer.

Research

We explored successfully established loyalty programs such as Student Price Card (SPC) and PC Optimum to identify best practices in user engagement and branding

Stakeholder Interviews

We consulted Rogers mentors to align on business objectives and gather insights into existing challenges.

I was primarily inspired by Student Price Card's marketing strategy. Every year during Welcome Week, they set up a booth on campus targeting students, who are already eligible for the program. Their goal was to drive sign-ups through an engaging on-site presence. When I came across their booth, I was intrigued enough to download the app and register on the spot—and I ended up winning a prize. That experience left a strong, positive impression on me and showed how effective games and prizes can be in driving engagement and brand loyalty.

Our Solution

Our team developed a user-centric solution that seamlessly integrates the 'Beyond the Seat' program into Rogers' digital ecosystem. By aligning the program's offerings with Rogers' brand identity, we aimed to foster customer loyalty through engaging and memorable experiences. This approach builds positive emotional connections, encouraging customers to associate these exclusive perks with the value of being part of the Rogers community.

Key Ideas

  • Partner with lifestyle influencers to increase the program's visibility among the target demographic.
  • Dedicate a page on the Rogers website to improve accessibility to the program's information.
  • Provide real-time notifications for contests, prizes, and events while offering exclusive in-app live streams with a trial of Rogers' 5G data.
  • Set up interactive booths in high-traffic locations like universities and transit stations, featuring games, exclusive prizes, VR experiences, and 5G demos.
