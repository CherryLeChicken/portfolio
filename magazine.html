Magazine

Course: GBDA 101 - Introduction to Digital Media Design
Date: April 2024

Tasked with designing a magazine that showcases the three projects from the GBDA 101 design course. I used Adobe Illustrator to create the visual elements and Adobe InDesign to assemble the final layout.

Assignment 1: Logo Design

I created a logo based on a classmate’s personality by researching their traits and translating them into visual design elements. The result was a personalized brand mark that reflected their identity.

Assignment 2: Horror-Themed Diptych Posters

Using self-taken photographs of Waterloo’s Village 1 residence, I designed a diptych that captured a horror theme, which embodied my first impressions of the residence. This project helped me improve my photography, composition, and visual storytelling skills.

Assignment 3: Designing an Emotion

I was assigned the emotion "delight". I brainstormed, prototyped, and tested designs that evoked joy. I explored how color and form can be used intentionally to create emotional responses in users.
