sCanadian

With a partner, I developed sCanadian during the 2025 Hack Canada hackathon. sCanadian is a web application that uses QuaggaJS to scan product barcodes and identify Canadian-made items. Using Ollama, it uses AI to provide recommendations which suggest Canadian alternatives.

Summary

The design prompt for Hack Canada 2025 was to solve a Canadian problem. We immediately thought of the current political situation with tarriffs incoming from the USA. We thought of building a tool that Canadians could easily use while shopping to ensure that they purchase Canadian products only. From then, sCanadian was born.

Key Features:

  • Barcode scanning: using the device's camera
  • Product identification: determines whether the food product is Canadian or not
  • Canadian alternatives using AI: provides 3 Canadian alternatives if a product is not Canadian
